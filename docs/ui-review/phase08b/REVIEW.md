# Phase 8B review checklist

Compare `ui/editorial-p08b-live` against approved `ui/editorial-p08a-predraft`; do not merge main yet.

1. Open a league's live draft and refresh. Check reported next pick, your slot/next pick and snapshot age against Sleeper. A receipt means uncached browser reads, not guaranteed provider real time.
2. Check the primary candidate and two alternatives. Expand one row, then inspect Advanced and Player details. Canonical league rank and current decision rank have different purposes; utility/index fields are not fantasy-point forecasts.
3. Browse available board and market values. Picked candidates must be absent. Search, position filters and Show more must work. Missing ADP belongs on the board, not invented in market values.
4. Inspect acquired assets, complete roster/legal assignment and observed draft log. Unresolved picked IDs must remain explicit. Slot counts do not imply a legal FLEX/SUPER_FLEX/IDP assignment.
5. Open the original Advanced pick optimizer/scenarios and return to Basic. Wait more than 30 seconds without refresh: timing advice must disappear, including old open evidence or Advanced scenario labels. Refresh restores only matching valid context.
6. Switch roster and league rapidly. No previous league's live advice should remain. Paused, missing slot, conflicting ledger, refresh error or current-season traded picks must visibly withhold timing.
7. Compare desktop and phone. Basic tables should remain digestible; explanations stay collapsed by default and source records stay in a native reader.
8. Confirm pre-draft 8A remains unchanged and completed draft review stays original (8C is next).

Browser screenshots and comparison JSON are fictional fixtures, not evidence of a real league's live draft. Candidate CI will rerun them after your branch push.
