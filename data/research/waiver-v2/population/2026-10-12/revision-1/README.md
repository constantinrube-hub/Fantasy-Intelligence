## Proof and limits

Published source hashes are replayed before derivation. All scheduled game identities, canonical franchise names, PBP actor coverage, published-stat identities, end clocks and final scoreboards are checked. Source actor occurrence anywhere in the NFL week blocks absence zeros conservatively, including deleted and non-scoring plays. The copied derivative validator checks certificates, anchors, exact calendar labels, unchanged forecasts and corrected nested fits. It does not independently reparse the full raw PBP; that derivation and its source hashes are recorded separately.

The two legacy roster-backed no-game team-weeks are BUF/CIN in 2022 Week 17 (the cancelled game); the old `CONFIRMED_BYE` descriptor is preserved, but no ordinary bye roster membership is assumed. Global point-score absence proofs need no guessed future team or roster transaction.
